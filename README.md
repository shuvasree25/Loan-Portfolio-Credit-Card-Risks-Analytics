# Loan-Portfolio-Credit-Card-Risks-Analytics
SQL-based loan portfolio and credit risk analysis using PostgreSQL, covering loan exposure, defaults, payment performance, delinquency, and collateral risk.
Project Overview
This project analyzes a loan portfolio using PostgreSQL and SQL to identify financial exposure, credit risk, payment issues, delinquency, and collateral gaps.
The analysis demonstrates how SQL can support portfolio monitoring, risk assessment, and securitization-related loan pool analysis.
Business Objectives
- Measure total loan portfolio and outstanding exposure.
- Analyze exposure by loan type and geographic region.
- Evaluate default rates and default exposure.
- Analyze credit-score segments and customer-level risk.
- Measure payment performance and payment shortfalls.
- Analyze delinquency and Days Past Due (DPD).
- Evaluate collateral coverage and Loan-to-Value (LTV).
- Identify high-LTV and under-collateralized loans.
- Identify loans with multiple risk indicators.
Key Business Questions
Portfolio & Credit Risk:
- What is the total loan portfolio and outstanding exposure?
- Which loan types and states have the highest exposure?
- What is the overall default rate and default exposure?
- Which credit-score segments show higher default rates?
- Which customers have the highest credit exposure?
Payment & Delinquency:
- What is the overall payment rate?
- What is the total payment shortfall?
- Which loans have repeated late payments?
- What is the average Days Past Due?
- Which loans have severe delinquency?
Collateral & LTV:
- What is the total collateral value?
- What is the collateral coverage ratio?
- Which loans have high LTV?
- Which loans are under-collateralized?
- Which loans have outstanding exposure without collateral?
Key Risk Indicators:
- Outstanding Exposure
- Default Rate
- Default Exposure
- Payment Shortfall
- Days Past Due
- Collateral Coverage
- Loan-to-Value (LTV)
- Credit Score Risk
- High-Exposure Loans
- Missing Collateral
Business Impact:
- Monitors overall portfolio exposure and outstanding balances.
- Identifies high-risk loans and customers.
- Highlights default and delinquency risk.
- Detects payment shortfalls and repeated payment delays.
- Identifies collateral gaps and high-LTV loans.
- Highlights geographic and customer-level concentration risk.
- Supports prioritization of loans with multiple risk indicators.
- Demonstrates SQL-based analysis for loan pool and securitization-related risk review.
Business Recommendations:
- Review high-exposure and high-LTV loans.
- Investigate significant payment shortfalls and repeated delays.
- Review under-collateralized and missing-collateral loans.
- Monitor customers with multiple risky loans.
- Monitor geographic concentration and credit-risk segments.
- Prioritize loans combining high exposure, high LTV, and delinquency indicators.
